NukeRat lets you set up port forwarding through compromised hosts so you can reach a specific port on the internal network.

The team server binds the listen port. Connections are forwarded to the internal host and port through the implant you select.

## How to configure

To create a port forward, follow the steps below:

  <Steps>
    <Step>
      ### Open the Network window
      Go to **NukeRat** > **Network**, then click **Add**.
    </Step>
    <Step>
      ### Configure the port forwarding
      <ImageZoom src={socks} alt="Port forward add" className="h-[15rem] tutorial_img" style={{ marginLeft: 0, marginRight: 0, display: 'block' }} />
      - **Team server**: listener API endpoint (host and port at the top of the form)
      - **Type**: `port forwarding`
      - **Host tag**: implant that will open connections to the internal target
      - **Listen port**: port opened on the team server
      - **Protocol**: TCP only (this is what the team server accepts today)
      - **Forward host**: internal IP or hostname to reach through the implant
      - **Forward port**: internal port to reach through the implant

      Click **Create** when the fields are set.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

Once done, your port forward should appear in the Network table. Connect to `<team_server_host>:<listen_port>` to reach `forward_host:forward_port` through the implant.
